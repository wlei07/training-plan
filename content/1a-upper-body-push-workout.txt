FLAT BENCH BARBELL PRESS
6 reps
3 sets (last set rest/pause system)
Rest between sets: 75–90 sec.
! Rest/Pause System: On the last set, perform 6 reps, rest for 10–12 seconds, push for 3–4 more reps with the same weight, rest for another 15 seconds, push for 2–3 more reps with the same weight, and finish the set.

STANDING MILITARY PRESS
8 reps
3 sets
Rest between sets: 60–75 sec.
! Tempo = 3/2/1/2: Lower the bar to chin level over 3 seconds, pause at chin level for 2 seconds, press up controlled yet explosively in 1 second, hold overhead for 2 seconds, and repeat.

INCLINE BENCH DUMBBELL FLY
8–10 rep range
3 sets
Rest between sets: 60–75 sec.
! Tempo = 2/3/1/2: Lower/open the dumbbells to the sides over 2 seconds, hold for 3 seconds at the peak chest stretch, close in a controlled 1 second, and squeeze your chest muscles hard for 2 seconds at the top!

INCLINE BENCH ONE ARM LATERAL RAISE
10–12 rep range
3 sets per arm
Rest between sets: 45–60 sec.

BARBELL SKULL CRUSHERS
8 reps
3 sets (last set rest/pause system)
Rest between sets: 60–75 sec.
! Rest/Pause System: Same system as the Bench Press!

SINGLE ARM ROPE PUSHDOWN
8 reps
3 sets (last set 8-10-12 drop set!)
Rest between sets: 60–75 sec.
! 8-10-12 Drop Set: On the last set, complete 8 reps, reduce the weight by 30–35% and push for 10 more reps, then reduce the weight by another 30–35% and push for 12 more reps before switching to the other arm.
