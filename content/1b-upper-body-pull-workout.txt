ONE ARM DUMBBELL ROW
6 reps
3 sets (last set rest/pause system)
Rest between sets: 75–90 sec.
! Rest/Pause System: On the last set, after 6 reps put the dumbbell down, rest for 10–12 seconds, then push for 3–4 more reps with the same dumbbell, put it down once more, rest for 15 seconds, push for 2–3 more reps with the same dumbbell, and switch to the other arm.

INCLINE BENCH BARBELL HIGH ROW
8–10 rep range
3 sets
Rest between sets: 60–75 sec.
! On every set, pull the bar toward your chest and squeeze your shoulder blades together.

BARBELL PULLOVER FOR LATS
10–12 rep range
3 sets
Rest between sets: 60 sec.
! Tempo = 2/2/2/1: Lower the bar overhead over 2 seconds, hold for 2 seconds at the point of full muscle stretch, pull the bar back to chest level over 2 seconds, pause for 1 second, and repeat.

BENT OVER REVERSE FLY
12–15 rep range
3 sets
Rest between sets: 45 sec.

CONCENTRATION CURL
8 reps
3 sets (last set 8-10-12 drop set!)
Rest between sets: 60–75 sec.
! 8-10-12 Drop Set: On the last set, after 8 reps reduce the dumbbell weight by 30–35% and do 10 more reps, then without resting reduce the weight by another 30–35% and do 12 more reps before switching to the other arm.

INCLINE BENCH HAMMER CURL
8–10 rep range
3 sets
Rest between sets: 60 sec.
