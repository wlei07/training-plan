BARBELL HIP THRUST
6 reps
3 sets (last set rest/pause system)
Rest between sets: 90 sec.
! Rest/Pause System: On the last set, after 6 reps sit down on the floor and rest for 10–12 seconds, push for 3–4 more reps with the same weight, then rest for another 15 seconds and push for 2–3 more reps with the same weight to finish.

DUMBBELL WALKING LUNGE

LEG CURL (single leg)
8 reps
3 sets (last set 8-10-12 drop set)
Rest between sets: 60 sec.
! 8-10-12 Drop Set: On the last set, after 8 reps reduce the weight by 30–35% and do 10 more reps, then without resting reduce the weight by another 30–35% and push for 12 more reps before switching to the other leg.
